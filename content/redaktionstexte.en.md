## Workflow Step 1

Title: Find a fitting license

Text: A short questionnaire helps identify which Creative Commons license fits best.

### Question 1: Attribution

Question: Should reuse show who created the material?

Info: When others use the material, an attribution notice can name you or your organization. Without this requirement, the release is as open as possible.

Answer A: Yes, name me or my organization

Answer B: No, release without attribution

### Question 2: Adaptation

Question: May others adapt your material?

Info: Adaptation includes translation, shortening, remixing, or adapting for a course.

Answer A: Yes, adaptation should be possible

Answer B: No, share unchanged only

### Question 3: Commercial Use

Question: May your material be used commercially?

Info: Commercial use does not only mean that someone sells the material. It can also become relevant when material is used in paid education, for example by adult education centres, private universities, tutoring services, or continuing education providers. Often the paid service is the issue, not the sale of the material itself.

Answer A: Yes, that is fine

Answer B: No, non-commercial only

### Question 4: Same License for Adaptations

Question: Should adaptations use the same license?

Info: Adaptations must be shared under the same license. This keeps adapted versions openly reusable as well.

Answer A: No, not necessary

Answer B: Yes, open should stay open
